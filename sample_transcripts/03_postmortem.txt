Post-mortem — "Finance close delayed 2 days"
Date: 2026-09-14
Attendees: Dana, Marcus, Priya, Rachel (finance systems)

RACHEL: From our side the impact was that month-end close slipped by two days. The
revenue numbers we pulled on the 1st were incomplete and we didn't catch it until
reconciliation on the 3rd.

MARCUS: Dana, what's the technical story?

DANA: Warehouse loads are backing up overnight. The batch window starts at 01:00 and
it's supposed to finish by 05:00, but over the last three weeks it's been running past
09:00. Once it runs long, the downstream aggregation jobs start on partial data
instead of waiting, so the numbers look complete but aren't.

RACHEL: That's the part that worries me most, honestly. Not that it was late — that it
was silently wrong. We had no way to tell the difference between a finished load and
a half-finished one.

DANA: That's fair.

MARCUS: Hold on. This is the third time we've had an action item about this pipeline
in about six weeks. August retro, sprint 42, now this.

DANA: It keeps coming back because we keep fixing the symptom. Last time it was the
alerting, before that it was the timeout window. The actual problem is the whole thing
is one monolithic batch job that can't be resumed or checkpointed.

MARCUS: OK. Then let's stop patching. Dana, action item: write a design doc for
checkpointing the warehouse batch pipeline so a partial run is detectable and
resumable. Two weeks — the 23rd.

DANA: That I can do properly.

MARCUS: Rachel, in the meantime, can finance hold off on pulling numbers until there's
a signal the load actually completed?

RACHEL: If there's a signal, yes. Right now there isn't one.

MARCUS: Dana, can you add a completion marker as a stopgap? Something finance can
check. By the 12th.

DANA: Yeah, that's a small change.

PRIYA: I'd also like us to add the warehouse batch to the on-call dashboard. It's not
on there at all right now, which is why nobody noticed it running long.

MARCUS: Priya, take that one. By the 16th.

PRIYA: Got it.
